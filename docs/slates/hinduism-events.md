1. Default: `flood-hinduism-1996` plus `britannica` s.v. the event title on
   every row.

   `akbar-dialogues-1575`, and `babri-demolition-1992` (all [hinduism, islam]).
   Their summaries are written in the project's neutral register and must land
   verbatim.
3. Composition-era texts use precision era or century as encoded; none are
   contested rows. No contested rows in this slate.
