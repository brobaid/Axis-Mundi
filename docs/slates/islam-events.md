## Sourcing rules for this slate

1. Default: `nchi-2010` plus `britannica` s.v. the event title on every row (the
   New Cambridge History's six volumes span the whole period).
2. `partition-of-india-1947` is multi-tradition [islam, hinduism, sikhism]; it
   adds `flood-hinduism-1996` and `mcleod-sikhism-1997`.
3. Belief-anchored events (`occultation-of-the-twelfth-imam-874`) use attribution
   framing in the summary per spec 9.3; they are not contested rows, since the
   record describes the tradition's account as its account.
4. No contested rows in this slate.
