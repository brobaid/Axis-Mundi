# Map Snapshot Memo: 1500 CE

Destination: `/docs/slates/snapshot-1500-memo.md`, with `/docs/slates/ce-1500.geojson`
as its output. Authored by the owner's reviewer. Fifth live era; completes the
originally scoped four historical frames.

## Method and rulings

1. Ninety-seven whole-unit realms, no cut geometries. Russia renders
   Christianity grade C as one unit: the Orthodox Muscovite core with the
   Muslim khanates of the Volga and the unconverted north and east inside one
   border, and the C hatch carrying that honesty rather than a cut line
   inventing a frontier the sources don't fix.
2. **Ruled is not majority, and this frame proves the distinction.** The
   Ottoman Balkans render Christian: Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece, Macedonia,
   Montenegro, and Romania are Christian-majority lands under Muslim rule, and
   the map colors populations, never thrones. Bosnia and Albania render Islam
   grade C, where conversion is genuinely advancing. Turkey renders Islam grade
   B, Muslim-majority Anatolia with its great Christian communities. The era
   note states the principle.
3. **Headline: the eve of everything.** Luther posts the theses seventeen
   years after this frame; Latin Christendom renders here unified for the last
   time. Granada fell eight years before it, closing eight centuries of
   al-Andalus, with the expulsions of Jews and Muslims in the same decade. The
   Americas render outside scope, their own religious worlds intact, with
   Columbus eight years landed; by the next live detent they are transformed.
   Christian Nubia is in its final years as the Funj rise. Iran is Sunni in
   this frame and Shia in the next, the Safavid conversion beginning the
   following year. The era note carries all five.
4. Southeast Asia's two turns render mid-motion: the Theravada turn complete
   on the mainland (Ayutthaya, post-Angkor Cambodia, Lan Xang all Buddhist A)
   and the Islamic turn advancing in the archipelago (Malacca B, Java C).
   Korea renders as the cluster at B: Joseon's Neo-Confucian order is
   suppressing Buddhism, a rare case of the cluster expanding by state policy.
   Mongolia renders outside scope: its second conversion is eight decades away.
5. Sources per the standing era map: Christianity at this frame cites
   macculloch-reformation-2003; Islam nchi-2010; others unchanged, encoded per
   feature.

## Era note (owner copy, verbatim)

time, seventeen years before the theses; Granada has just fallen, ending eight
centuries of al-Andalus, and the same decade's expulsions scatter Sephardi
