## Rulings for this slate

1. Default sources: `hardacre-shinto-2017` plus `britannica` s.v. the title.
2. **Band-floor waiver (owner ruling):** applies as in the Sikhism and Jainism
   slates; totals below the rubric floors are by design.
3. Attribution rows (archaeological attestation, Ise's emergence) frame their
   dating explicitly; no contested rows. The Yasukuni row's note is political
   fact, not a contested flag.
