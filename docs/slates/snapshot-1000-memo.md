# Map Snapshot Memo: 1000 CE

Destination: `/docs/slates/snapshot-1000-memo.md`, with `/docs/slates/ce-1000.geojson`
as its output. Authored by the owner's reviewer. Fourth live era, and the first
with cut geometries.

## Method and rulings

1. Seventy-eight realms: seventy-five whole Natural Earth units plus three cut
   features. Cut 1 splits Iberia at roughly the Douro-Ebro line (41.3°N):
   al-Andalus at Almanzor's height renders Islam grade A south of it, the
   Christian kingdoms render grade A north of it. Cut 2 carves the Kievan Rus
   realm, Christianity grade C, twelve years after the baptism of 988, from the
   Ukraine-Belarus-Russia union; the rest of the steppe and forest north stays
   outside scope, honestly pagan. Both cut lines are scholarly conventions and
   the cartouche's caveat covers them.
2. Christianity at this era cites macculloch-christianity-2009 per the era
   chain; Islam cites nchi-2010; others per the standing T1 map, encoded per
   feature.
3. **Headline: the Islamic ecumene at its classical meridian**, unbroken from
   al-Andalus to the Indus, with Cairo, Baghdad, and Cordoba its poles. Grade B
   where great non-Muslim populations persist (Egypt's Copts, the Levant's
   churches), C at the conversion frontiers (Punjab under Ghaznavid raids, the
   Kazakh steppe).
4. **The quiet tragedy the scrubber shows: Zoroastrianism has vanished from its
   own map.** Iran renders Islam grade A; between the 600 and 1000 detents the
   fires' realm becomes minority everywhere, surviving in Yazd, Kerman, and
   Gujarat below majority resolution. The era note carries this, and the 600-to-
   1000 crossfade is the museum's single most eloquent transition.
5. Northern conversions in progress, all grade C: Poland (966), Hungary
   (crowned 1000 exactly), Denmark, Norway, Iceland (converted at the althing
   this very year), Kievan Rus. Sweden, Finland, and the Baltic render outside
   scope: their conversion is one to two centuries away.
6. East and South: Song China as the cluster at A; Goryeo Korea Buddhist A;
   Heian Japan Shinto B under deep kami-buddha amalgamation, per the standing
   convention with the note; Chola-era India Hinduism A; Pala Bengal Buddhist C;
   Angkor Cambodia and Mataram Java Hindu B, the Southeast Asian Hindu moment
   before the Theravada and Islamic turns.
7. Still no Judaism realm, and now no Zoroastrian one: the two oldest
   traditions on the site hold no majority anywhere at the millennium, which
   the era note states in one line each.

## Era note (owner copy, verbatim)

"At the millennium the Islamic ecumene runs unbroken from al-Andalus to the
Indus, the largest civilization the map has yet shown. Christendom is
converting its own north: Poland, Hungary, Scandinavia, and Kievan Rus all
render hatched, faiths a generation old. And two absences structure everything:
Zoroastrianism, which colored this plate's whole center four centuries ago, now
holds no majority anywhere, its fires burning on in Yazd, Kerman, and Gujarat
below the resolution of a map like this; and Jewish life, at its medieval
height in Cordoba and Baghdad, remains what it has been on every plate since
the first: everywhere present, majority nowhere."
