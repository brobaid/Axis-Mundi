# Map Snapshot Memos: The Final Seven Eras

Destination: `/docs/slates/snapshots-batch-memo.md`, governing seven geojson
outputs committed alongside it: `ce-bce-0500`, `ce-0300`, `ce-0750`, `ce-1200`,
`ce-1700`, `ce-1850`, `ce-1950`. Authored by the owner's reviewer. Standing
conventions from the five live eras apply throughout: whole Natural Earth
units except where a cut is named, scholarly-convention borders, ruled-is-not-
majority everywhere, era-appropriate Christianity T1s as encoded, the France
South American clip applied to every pre-1700 frame per the standing exception,
and the tradition id `chinese` per the taxonomy. Each era note below is owner
copy, verbatim, in the standing treatment.

## 500 BCE (12 realms)

The axial age, and the sparsest plate: Confucius, the Buddha, and Mahavira are
alive in this frame, Zoroaster's hymns already old, the Second Temple sixteen
years dedicated. India renders grade C because the frame's whole point is that
its future is undecided among Brahmanic, Buddhist, and Jain currents. Sri
Lanka, Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia render outside or empty: their entries
into these traditions are centuries ahead.

Era note: "This is the axial plate. Confucius is teaching in Lu, the Buddha in
Magadha, Mahavira among the same cities; the Second Temple is newly dedicated
and the Gathas already old. Nearly everything on later plates descends from
the handful of colors here, and nearly all the world is still outside them."

## 300 CE (19 realms)

Armenia is the headline: converted in 301, the first Christian state on any
plate, grade B for its freshness, while Christianity holds no other majority
anywhere twelve years before Milan. Gandhara renders Buddhist at its artistic
peak. Japan enters the map for the first time at grade C, on archaeological
attestation alone.

Era note: "At 300 Christianity is everywhere present and majority almost
nowhere: one kingdom, Armenia, has crossed first, and the empire that will
follow in a generation still persecutes. The Sasanian fires burn at full
state strength, and Gandhara's workshops are carving the Buddha's face for
the first civilizations that will carry it east."

## 750 CE (60 realms)

The conversion-curve plate, and the museum's boldest use of its own principle:
the caliphate rules from Spain to Sindh, but scholarship on conversion places
Muslim majorities only in Arabia at this date, so Iran renders Zoroastrian
grade B under Abbasid rule, Syria, Lebanon, and Egypt render Christian grade
B, al-Andalus renders Christian grade B, and Iraq alone among the conquered
lands renders Islam, at grade C, for its garrison cities. Khazaria's elite
conversion to Judaism is deliberately not rendered: the scholarship is too
contested to color a realm, and the era note says so.

Era note: "In 750 the caliphate rules from the Atlantic to the Indus, and
most of its subjects are not Muslims. Conversion is a curve, not a conquest:
Iran still keeps the fires, Syria and Egypt still fill the churches, and
al-Andalus prays in Latin under Arab rule. This plate colors practice, and
practice lags power by centuries. One realm is deliberately absent: the
Khazar elite's adoption of Judaism is attested, contested, and too uncertain
to paint."

## 1200 CE (88 realms, one cut)

High medieval equilibrium: Christendom converted to its northern edges except
pagan Lithuania and the crusade-frontier Baltics at C, Byzantium's Anatolia
still Christian-majority grade B under Seljuk rule a century after Manzikert,
the Almohad cut holding southern Iberia a decade before Las Navas, Saladin's
Jerusalem back under Islam, Jayavarman VII's Cambodia at its Mahayana moment,
and Pagan Myanmar at apex. Mongolia renders outside scope in the very frame
Chinggis unites it: the power about to redraw every border on this plate
worships outside its scope.

Era note: "1200 is the high medieval balance a single generation will
overturn. Anatolia still prays in Greek under Turkish rule; al-Andalus holds
its last half-peninsula; Jerusalem has returned to Islam; and in the one
gray space that matters most, the steppe, Chinggis Khan has just united the
power that will redraw everything, worshipping sky and ancestors outside
every color this museum carries."

## 1700 CE (129 realms)

Westphalia's world: the confessional map of Europe settled, the Americas
colored Christian at grade B for the syncretic, coerced, and incomplete
conversions the grade honestly carries, with the North American colonies at C
against an indigenous interior the plate does not pretend is converted.
Mongolia flips Buddhist between 1500 and this frame, the second conversion.
Bengal renders its new Muslim majority. Sudan's Christian Nubia is gone, five
detents after it entered.

Era note: "By 1700 the map has crossed oceans. The Americas render Christian
in grades that carry their truth: conversion real, coerced, syncretic, and
incomplete, deepest in the viceroyalties and thinnest at the frontiers. Old
Christian Nubia has left the plate after a thousand years; Mongolia has
turned Buddhist; Bengal has crossed to a Muslim majority; and Europe's colors
are now confessional facts fixed by treaty."

## 1850 CE (134 realms)

The colonial-era plate before the scramble: the Americas at grade A as settler
and republican Christianity consolidates, Australia and New Zealand entering
at B, Greece independent, the Sokoto caliphate holding northern Nigeria at C,
and Asia's colors steady under colonial rule that the plate, by principle,
does not paint. The Taiping war burns in this frame's China without changing
its color.

Era note: "1850 colors a world where power is European almost everywhere and
practice is not. India renders Hindu under the Raj, China Confucian-Taoist
mid-Taiping, Africa's interior keeps its own religions between an Islamic
north and mission footholds at the coasts. The next plate will show what the
century between them did."

## 1950 CE (147 realms)

The postwar plate, and the scrubber's most moving arrival: Israel renders
Judaism grade A, the first Judaism realm since Judea at 1 CE, nineteen
centuries and nine detents after it left the map, two years after the state's
founding and five after the Shoah. The Soviet bloc renders at reduced grades
under antireligious campaign, China at C in the PRC's second year, Mongolia at
C after the purges. Partition's twin states render their first frame. Albania
approaches its coming atheist-state decree at B.

Era note: "In 1950 the map answers its oldest absence: Israel renders as the
first Judaism realm since Judea, nineteen centuries after the legions left
this plate's second frame, five years after the catastrophe. Around it the
century's other forces show as grades: the Soviet campaigns dim a
continent's east, China begins its suppression years, and the partitioned
subcontinent renders its two new states. The next detent is the present."
