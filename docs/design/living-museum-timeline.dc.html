Christianity 325 CE · Council · Rank 5

First Council of Nicaea

Exhibit label
Convened byConstantine I
LocationNicaea, Anatolia
TypeEcumenical council

First ecumenical council of the Christian church. It produced the original Nicene Creed and condemned Arianism, setting the pattern for conciliar authority that shaped every later schism and settlement.
