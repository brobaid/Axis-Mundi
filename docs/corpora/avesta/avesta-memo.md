# Reading Room Corpus: The Avesta

Destination: `/docs/corpora/avesta/`. Authored by the owner's reviewer.

1. English: the Sacred Books of the East translations, Mills for the Yasna,
   Darmesteter for the Vendidad, public domain (new source record
   `sbe-avesta`, on-page note: "L. H. Mills and James Darmesteter's Sacred
   Books of the East translations (1880-1887), public domain; their register
   is Victorian and their readings a century behind current scholarship,
   stated plainly").
2. English-only, and the note is structural: no public-domain digitization of
   the Avestan original exists in reachable sources; the original slot renders
   "The Avestan text is not yet available from a public-domain source; this
   room is English-only for now." This is the reverse of the Guru Granth
   Sahib's situation and uses the same honest-slot treatment.
3. Two divisions delivered: the Yasna, 72 chapters, with chapters 28-34,
   43-51, and 53 badged as the Gathas, the oldest stratum, attributed by the
   tradition to Zarathustra himself; and the Vendidad, 22 fargards. The
   corpus's own division notes carry both statements. The dive's canon row
   already states that roughly a quarter of the Sasanian Avesta survives;
   the shelf entry repeats it in one line so the reader knows this room is a
   remnant by history, not by editing.
