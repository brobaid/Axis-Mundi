# Reading Room Corpus: The Kojiki

Destination: `/docs/corpora/kojiki/`. Authored by the owner's reviewer.

1. English: Basil Hall Chamberlain's 1882 translation, public domain (new
   source record `chamberlain-1882`, on-page note: "Basil Hall Chamberlain's
   1882 translation, public domain; Chamberlain renders proper names by their
   meanings, so deities appear as translated epithets"). English-only with the
   standing original-slot note; the Old Japanese text awaits a public-domain
   digitization.
2. Per the Shinto dive's canon row, the Kojiki enters as chronicle, not
   scripture: the shelf entry and the room's header both carry "a chronicle
   revered, not a scripture revealed" so the Reading Room does not silently
   promote it.
3. 170 of Chamberlain's 180 sections are delivered; ten (15, 18, 61, 122,
   136, 140, 141, 152, 159, 174) are absent from the source digitization and
   are listed by number on the contents page as gaps in the source, not in
   the work. A future source delivery may patch them.
