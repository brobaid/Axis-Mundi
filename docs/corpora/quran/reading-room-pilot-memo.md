# Reading Room: Scope Ruling and Quran Pilot

Destination: `/docs/corpora/quran/` (this memo plus `quran-paired.json`).
Authored by the owner's reviewer. Opens Phase 5.

## The licensing reality, stated once

Original-language scriptures are public domain. Modern English translations
mostly are not. The Reading Room therefore uses public-domain and CC0 editions,
named on every page, accepting archaic register where that is the price of
clean rights. Each edition becomes a source record; text pages are sourced
content like everything else.

## Pilot corpus: the Quran

`quran-paired.json`: 6,236 verses, Arabic Uthmani text (Hafs reading, Tanzil
text via the CC0 quran-api packaging) paired with Pickthall's 1930 translation,
public domain in the United States as of 2025. New source records:
`tanzil-uthmani-hafs` (T1, the standard digital Arabic text) and
`pickthall-1930` (T1 for this purpose, with the on-page note: "Marmaduke
Pickthall's 1930 translation, public domain; its register is deliberately
archaic").
