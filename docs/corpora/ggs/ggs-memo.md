# Reading Room Corpus: The Guru Granth Sahib

Destination: `/docs/corpora/ggs/`. Authored by the owner's reviewer. The final
corpus of the acquisition list; the Reading Room's first delivery cycle closes
with it.

1. Gurmukhi: the complete text, all 1,430 angs, 60,555 lines, assembled from
   the ShabadOS database's primary source text (MIT-licensed digitization of
   the public-domain scripture; new source record `shabados-sggs`, on-page
   note: "The Gurmukhi text as digitized by the ShabadOS project"). The ang
   is the tradition's own citation unit and is the route and citable unit
   here: /read/guru-granth-sahib/<ang>.
2. Original-only by standing ruling, and this corpus is why that mode exists:
   no public-domain English translation of quality exists. The English slot
   renders the honest waiting-on note: "An open English translation is not
   yet available to this museum; the Gurmukhi stands alone until one is."
   The language toggle presents Original as the only live state, with
   English and Both disabled carrying that sentence as their title. The
   Devotional frame hook remains available for owner-supplied conventions
   in a later delivery.
3. Typography is the deliverable here: Gurmukhi renders in the design
   language's Gurmukhi face at a reading size tuned for its vowel signs and
   subjoined letters, lines kept as the source's line divisions, never
   re-flowed into paragraphs, since lineation is part of how this scripture
   is read and cited. Ang 1 opens with the Mool Mantar as the text's own
   first line, no added frame.
4. The Sikhism dive's canon row links here and its "one eternal Guru" claim
   now has the full text behind it; the shelf entry states plainly that this
   is the only room in the library whose scripture is present whole in its
   original and absent whole in translation, which is itself a fact about
   the state of open scholarship worth displaying.
