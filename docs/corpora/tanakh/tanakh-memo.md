# Reading Room Corpus: Tanakh

Destination: `/docs/corpora/tanakh/` (this memo plus `tanakh-paired.json`).
Authored by the owner's reviewer. Second corpus.

## Editions and rulings

1. Hebrew: the Westminster Leningrad Codex text with full nikkud (new source
   record `wlc-nikkud`, T1: the standard digital Masoretic text). English: the
   Jewish Publication Society's 1917 translation (new source record
   `jps-1917`, T1 for this purpose), public domain, with the on-page note:
   "The Jewish Publication Society's 1917 translation, public domain; its
   register is deliberately elevated."
2. Alignment: 39 books, 23,213 verses, paired verse-for-verse with zero
   mismatches; both editions carry the same versification, measured, not
   assumed. No single-sided verses in this corpus.
3. Order and grouping are the Tanakh's own, encoded in the file's book order:
   Torah (first 5 books), Nevi'im (next 21), Ketuvim (final 13). The reader
   renders these three section headings; the Christian Old Testament ordering
   is not used here and the Bible corpus, when it arrives, orders itself.
4. Verse text is the Hebrew joined from its word tokens; cantillation and
   vowel points are preserved as given.
