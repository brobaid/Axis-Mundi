# Tanakh Corpus v2: Re-delivery Note

Replaces v1 wholesale. Corrections against the v1 flags:

1. The English is now the unmodified JPS 1917 (Sefaria's faithful digitization,
   "The Holy Scriptures: A New Translation", LORD/God conventions as printed;
   two stray HaShem tokens remain from the source digitization and are left as
   received). The v1 file carried a modified observance-convention text and the
   memo's "zero mismatches" was the owner's measurement error; both are
   corrected here and the record stands.
2. The Hebrew is the Wikisource Tanach with cantillation, Masoretic
   versification, with Zephaniah 3 patched from the dump's merged Hebrew
   edition where the trope file was empty. New source records:
   `wikisource-tanach` (he) and `jps-1917` (en, unmodified note).
3. Honest residuals, counted: 23,206 verse slots, 2 Hebrew-only and 4
   English-only, confined to the known versification divergences (Exodus 20,
   Deuteronomy 5, Jeremiah 30, Psalm 70); each renders the standing
   single-sided treatment.
4. `book_he` now carries every book's Hebrew title from the source; section
   headings render bilingually: Torah תורה, Nevi'im נביאים, Ketuvim כתובים.
