# Reading Room Corpus: The Bible

Destination: `/docs/corpora/bible/`. Authored by the owner's reviewer.

1. English: the World English Bible, a modern public-domain translation (new
   source record `web-bible`, on-page note: "The World English Bible, a modern
   public-domain translation"). Greek: the SBL Greek New Testament (new source
   record `sblgnt`, note: "The SBL Greek New Testament, a critical edition,
   CC-licensed"). 66 books, 31,105 verses, Protestant canon and order; the
   deuterocanon is a later delivery and its absence is stated on the contents
   page, not silent.
2. The Old Testament is English-only by ruling: its Hebrew original lives in
   the Tanakh reading room in Masoretic order, and every OT book page carries
   that pointer as one line. The New Testament is paired verse-for-verse with
   the Greek.
3. Honest residuals, and this time they are a feature: 21 NT verses are
   English-only, and they are precisely the verses the critical Greek text
   does not carry (Matthew 17:21, 18:11, 23:14, Mark 7:16, 9:44, 9:46, 11:26,
   15:28, and their peers): each renders the single-sided treatment with the
   one-line note "This verse appears in the traditional text; the critical
   Greek edition does not carry it." Two verses are Greek-only. The corpus
   thereby displays text-critical history rather than hiding it.
