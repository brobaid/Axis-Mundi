# Reading Room Corpus: The Dhammapada

Destination: `/docs/corpora/dhammapada/`. Authored by the owner's reviewer.
Opens the Pali shelf.

1. Pali: the Mahasangiti edition as segmented by SuttaCentral (new source
   record `mahasangiti-bilara`). English: Bhikkhu Sujato's translation,
   dedicated to the public domain, CC0 (new source record `sujato-cc0`,
   on-page note: "Bhikkhu Sujato's translation, released without restriction;
   a rare modern public-domain scripture translation"). 26 vaggas, 423 verses,
   zero missing on either side.
2. Chapters carry Pali and English names as delivered (Yamakavagga / Pairs);
   routes by vagga number with the verse range shown (1-20). Verse anchors use
   the tradition's own continuous numbering, Dhp 1 through 423.
3. This corpus opens the Pali shelf, not completes it: the shelf entry states
   that the Dhammapada is one book of the Khuddaka Nikaya and that further
   nikayas arrive from the same CC0 corpus in later deliveries; the standing
   waiting-on treatment, scoped to a shelf that is already partly live.
